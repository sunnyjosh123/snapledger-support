瞬记账本支持 / SnapLedger Support

简体中文

欢迎使用瞬记账本。如果你在使用中遇到问题，可以先查看下方常见问题，或通过页面底部的方式联系我们。

常见问题

1. 账本数据会上传到云端吗？

当前版本默认本地保存账本、预算、分类和偏好数据，不主动上传到我们的服务器。

2. AI 管家需要联网吗？

当前版本默认本地优先。系统端侧能力不可用时，App 会使用内置轻量意图模型和本地规则回复，因此核心记账和轻量问答可离线使用。

3. 为什么订阅页提示无法连接 App Store 商品？

请确认网络连接正常，并已登录 Apple 账号。如问题持续，请稍后重试或联系我们。

4. 如何恢复购买？

进入设置页，打开 Pro/订阅入口，点击恢复购买。购买和订阅状态由 Apple App Store 管理。

5. 如何删除数据？

进入设置页，使用删除账号/清除本地数据功能。删除后，本机账单、预算和偏好将被清除。App Store 订阅仍需在 Apple 账号中管理。

English

Welcome to SnapLedger. If you run into a problem, check the FAQ below or contact us using the options at the bottom of this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does SnapLedger upload my ledger data to the cloud?

The current version stores ledger records, budgets, categories, and preferences locally by default. It does not actively upload this data to our servers.

2. Does the AI assistant require internet access?

The current version is local-first. When system on-device capabilities are unavailable, the app uses its bundled lightweight intent model and local rules, so core expense tracking and assistant replies work offline.

3. Why does the subscription screen say it cannot connect to App Store products?

Make sure you are online and signed in with your Apple Account. If the problem continues, try again later or contact us.

4. How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings, go to the Pro/subscription entry, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchase and subscription status is managed by the Apple App Store.

5. How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and use the delete account or clear local data option. This clears local bills, budgets, and preferences from the device. App Store subscriptions must still be managed through your Apple Account.
