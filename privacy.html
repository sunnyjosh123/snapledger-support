瞬记账本隐私政策 / SnapLedger Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-11-09

简体中文

瞬记账本尊重并保护你的隐私。本政策说明瞬记账本如何处理你的数据。

1. 本地账本数据

你在 App 中创建的支出记录、预算、分类、备注、扫描结果和偏好设置默认保存在你的设备本地。当前版本不会把这些账本数据上传到我们的服务器。

2. 相机、相册、麦克风与语音识别

当你使用拍照记账、相册导入或语音快记功能时，App 会请求相机、相册、麦克风和语音识别权限。这些权限仅用于完成你主动发起的记账操作。语音识别能力由 iOS 系统提供，实际处理方式可能受 Apple 系统服务设置影响。

3. Face ID

如果你启用 Face ID 保护，身份验证由 iOS 系统处理。瞬记账本不会获取或存储你的面容数据。

4. Apple 登录

如果你使用 Sign in with Apple，App 可能在本机保存 Apple 提供的用户标识、姓名或邮箱，用于显示账号状态。当前版本不会把这些信息上传到我们的服务器。

5. AI 管家

AI 管家用于回答你的消费和预算问题，全部在你的设备本地运行。

  • 处理的数据：仅在你主动打开并使用 AI 管家时，在设备本地读取本月总支出、分类支出比例、预算金额与剩余额度，以及你输入的问题。
  • 处理方式：优先使用 Apple 系统端侧模型；不可用时使用 App 内置的轻量意图模型和本地规则。所有处理均在设备上完成。
  • 第三方共享：本 App 不包含、不连接任何第三方 AI 服务（如 OpenAI、Anthropic、DeepSeek 等），也不向我们的服务器或任何第三方发送、共享或出售你的账本数据和提问内容。
  • 使用目的：仅用于在设备上生成消费复盘和回答你的问题，不用于广告、用户画像或跨 App 追踪。
  • 授权与撤回：首次使用 AI 管家前，App 会展示《AI 助手隐私与数据使用说明》并请求你同意。你可以随时在「设置」的 AI 管家卡片中查看说明或撤销授权，撤销后 AI 功能立即停用。

如果未来版本启用云端 AI 服务，我们会在启用前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私标签，说明传输的数据、接收方和用途，并再次征得你的同意。

6. 订阅与购买

订阅和购买由 Apple App Store 处理。App 会读取 App Store 返回的交易状态，以同步你的 Pro 权益。我们不会直接处理你的支付卡号或银行账户信息。

7. 第三方服务

当前版本不包含广告 SDK，不请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限，也不用于跨 App 或网站追踪。如未来启用分析、崩溃上报、后端代理或云端 AI 等第三方服务，我们会更新本政策和 App Store 隐私标签。

8. 删除数据

你可以在 App 设置中删除账号和本地数据。删除后，本机账单、预算和偏好将被清除。App Store 订阅仍需通过你的 Apple 账号管理。

9. 联系我们

如有隐私问题，请通过支持页面联系我们。

English

SnapLedger respects your privacy. This policy explains how SnapLedger handles your data.

1. Local Ledger Data

Expense records, budgets, categories, notes, receipt parsing results, and preferences you create in the app are stored on your device by default. The current version does not upload this ledger data to our servers.

2. Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech Recognition

When you use receipt capture, photo import, or voice logging, the app may request access to the camera, photo library, microphone, and speech recognition. These permissions are used only for actions you initiate. Speech recognition is provided by iOS system services, and processing behavior may depend on Apple system settings.

3. Face ID

If you enable Face ID protection, authentication is handled by iOS. SnapLedger does not access or store your facial biometric data.

4. Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, the app may store the Apple-provided user identifier, name, or email locally to display account status. The current version does not upload this information to our servers.

5. AI Assistant

The AI assistant answers questions about your spending and budgets, and runs entirely on your device.

  • Data used: only when you open and use the assistant, it reads on-device your monthly total spending, category breakdown, budget amount and remaining balance, and the question you type.
  • How it is processed: Apple's on-device model is used when available; otherwise the app's bundled lightweight intent model and local rules. All processing happens on your device.
  • Third parties: the app does not include or connect to any third-party AI service (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek), and does not send, share, or sell your ledger data or questions to our servers or any third party.
  • Purpose: only to generate spending reviews and answer your questions on your device; never for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.
  • Permission and revocation: before first use, the app shows an "AI Assistant Privacy & Data Use" disclosure and asks for your permission. You can review it or revoke permission at any time in Settings, in the AI assistant card; the assistant stops immediately when revoked.

If a future version enables cloud AI services, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy labels before enabling the feature, describe what data is sent, to whom, and why, and ask for your permission again.

6. Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. The app reads transaction status returned by the App Store to sync your Pro entitlement. We do not directly process your payment card number or bank account information.

7. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and is not used for tracking across apps or websites. If we enable analytics, crash reporting, backend proxy, or cloud AI services in the future, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy labels.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and local data in the app settings. After deletion, local bills, budgets, and preferences will be cleared from the device. App Store subscriptions must still be managed through your Apple Account.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us through the support page.
